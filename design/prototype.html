Your notes and AI agents

Bible CLI can let AI agents you connect read your study and save research back. Please choose. Nothing is pre-selected.

What’s shared

Your notes, research, highlights, plans and reading history can be read by agents you connect (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok Bot…).

Defaults

New notes are visible to agents unless you hide them. Connected agents get Full access: they can save notes, plans and research. You can change this per agent.

Why it’s sensitive

This material can reveal your religious beliefs, which data-protection law treats as sensitive personal data.

Third parties

Once an agent reads it, that platform’s own data policies apply.

You stay in control

Hide any note, change or revoke any agent, and withdraw this consent anytime in Settings → Agents.

Remain in Me — μένω in John 15

John 15:1–111 John 2:24John 6:56
Open conversation ↗ · visible to agents

OBSERVEDμένω occurs 10 times in John 15:1–11 (11 in the chapter; BSB: “remain”). The first is the imperative μείνατε in v.4: a command to the group.

OBSERVEDIn v.4b the Greek has no second verb — κἀγὼ ἐν ὑμῖν, “and I in you.” BSB supplies “will remain.”

PATTERNJohn uses μένω 40 times and 1 John 24 times — 64 of the NT’s 118. It is a signature word for the relationship between Jesus and his disciples.

INFERENCE“Remain” describes a continuing relationship more than a single act; fruit (v.5), answered prayer (v.7) and joy (v.11) follow from it.
